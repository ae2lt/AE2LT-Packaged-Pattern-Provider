# Terra Plate

**Packaged Core:** <ItemLink id="ae2ltpp:botania_terra_plate_packaged_core" />
**Mod:** Botania

Automatically performs Terrestrial Agglomeration. Place the provider against the Terra Plate and encode the corresponding **processing pattern**. The provider sends in the ingredients and automatically retrieves the result after crafting finishes.

## ⚠ Notes

* The Terra Plate needs a mana supply. Make sure enough mana is available.
* Before starting, the plate must be idle and its 1-block item area must have no dropped items in it.
