# Petal Apothecary

**Packaged Core:** <ItemLink id="ae2ltpp:botania_petal_apothecary_packaged_core" />
**Mod:** Botania

Automatically performs Petal Apothecary recipes. Place the provider against the Apothecary and encode the corresponding **processing pattern**; the provider automatically retrieves the crafted result.

## ⚠ Crafting consumption

Water must be supplied before each craft. Water sources are used in this order of priority: a water bucket in the pattern inputs (the empty bucket is returned), water supplied as a fluid in the pattern inputs, then water already in the Apothecary.
